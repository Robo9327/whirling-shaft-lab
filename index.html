On the Whirling of Shafts

Raj Pandya
Roll No. 24BME091
Batch A-5

Every rotor carries a little unbalance. At one particular speed that unbalance makes the shaft bow out violently: the critical speed. This laboratory builds the apparatus in three dimensions, derives the theory step by step, and then lets you run the experiment yourself, through resonance and beyond.
